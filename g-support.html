G Support

G is head-to-head golf for iMessage, made by Mountain Media Digital LLC. Play your hole, send it, and your rival plays the same hole back. Same wind, same ground.

How do I start a match?

Open any iMessage conversation, tap the + beside the text field, and choose G from the list. Pick your round, game, and course, play your hole, and send it. Your rival opens the bubble to watch your shots and play theirs.

My purchases aren't showing

Open the app → menu → Pro Shop → Restore Purchases. Unlocks belong to your Apple Account and restore on any of your devices. A course a friend sends you is always playable, owned or not. Owning a course lets you start matches on it.

Sound, haptics, and the Lock Screen banner

Everything is adjustable in the app under menu → Settings, including separate volume sliders for game sounds, course ambience, and the gallery.

Something's broken

Email us. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Screenshots help. We read everything.
