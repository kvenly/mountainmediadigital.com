The loop

One hole. Back and forth.

G is an iMessage app, so a round is just a conversation you're already having. Nothing to schedule, nothing to install for the person you're playing.

1

Open an iMessage thread

Tap the apps button beside the text field and pick the G. Choose three random holes, the front nine, or the back nine.

2

Play your hole and send it

A light, fast flick is the whole swing. When you finish the hole, the match travels inside the message bubble.

3

They play the same hole

Same wind, same ground, same everything. Then it comes back to you with their shots drawn over yours.

In the thread

It looks like a message, because it is one.

No app to open, no lobby to join. The match rides in the bubble: you play your hole and send it, they watch your shots replay over their own ground, then play it their way and send it back. Every course is free when a friend invites you.

Tap the apps button in any thread and pick the G.

Your hole becomes the message. Send it and the ball is in their court.

They open it and watch your round play out before taking their turn.
