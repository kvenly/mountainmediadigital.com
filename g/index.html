Golf Game G · for iMessage

Golf that lives in your iMessages.

Play your hole and send it. Your friend plays the same one and sends it back, inside the conversation you're already having.

Free to download · iPhone and iPad
The feel

Look at the hole. Then hit it.

Every hole opens with a flyover. One light flick up the screen is the whole swing, and the camera rides the ball out.

How it plays

One hole. Back and forth.

G is an iMessage app, so a round is just a conversation you're already having. Nothing to schedule. The round lives in the thread.

  1. 1

    Open an iMessage thread

    Tap the apps button beside the text field and pick G.

  2. 2

    Play your hole and send it

    A light flick is the whole swing. Finish the hole and it goes out as the message.

  3. 3

    They play the same hole

    It comes back to you with their shots drawn over yours. Your move.

Nine courses

Nine worlds, not nine reskins.

Every course has its own horizon, its own light, and its own sound: songbirds, rain in the pines, gulls over the wash, a city humming in the distance.

  • Free
    to play

The Gyre is free. Any other course is free to play when a friend who owns it starts the round.

Three formats

Pick your needle.

Each format speaks its own language on the card, and the bubble always says it in names, because both of you are reading the same message.

  • Stroke

    Lowest total wins the round. The honest one.

  • Match

    Hole by hole. Go three up with two to play and it's over early, dormie and all.

  • Skins

    Every hole is its own pot. Tie it and the pot carries. The pressure stacks up fast.
