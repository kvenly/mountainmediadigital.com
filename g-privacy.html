G Privacy Policy

Effective August 2026 · Mountain Media Digital LLC, La Conner, WA

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. G has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. There is no server behind the game. Your matches travel inside your own iMessage conversations.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, or contact information
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or crash reports
  • No advertising identifiers and no ads
  • No data from your contacts or conversations

Stored on your device

Your golfer's name and colors, your scores and match history, and your settings are stored only on your device (and in the app's shared container so the iMessage extension can read them). Deleting the app deletes this data. We never see it.

Matches travel through iMessage

When you play a hole and send it, your shot data, score, and golfer name travel inside the message bubble, delivered by Apple's iMessage, the same way a photo you text would be. Only the people in that conversation receive it. Mountain Media Digital has no server and never receives, stores, or can read your matches. iMessage is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Optional one-time unlocks (courses, game formats, colors) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment information. Purchases can be restored on any device signed into the same Apple Account, using Restore Purchases in the app.

Children's privacy

G does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app contains no ads, no links to social media within gameplay, and no data collection of any kind.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
