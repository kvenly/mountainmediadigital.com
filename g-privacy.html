G Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026 · Mountain Media Digital LLC, La Conner, WA

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. G has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. There is no server behind the game. Your matches travel inside your own iMessage conversations.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, or contact information
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or crash reports
  • No advertising identifiers and no ads
  • No data from your contacts or conversations

Stored on your device

Your golfer's name and colors, your scores and match history, and your settings are stored only on your device (and in the app's shared container so the iMessage extension can read them). Deleting the app deletes this data. We never see it.

Replays and Photos

G can make a short film of a hole you played. Here is exactly what that means.

What G keeps on your phone. When you save a hole to your Replays shelf, G keeps a small recipe for it: the course and hole, the day's wind, and your strokes (about 450 bytes), plus a small thumbnail picture. It also keeps whether you have made a film of it. These live on your phone, in the storage G shares between the app and its iMessage extension. They never leave your phone. G keeps up to 20 replays, plus your longest drive and your longest putt.

What happens when you tap render. G draws the hole again on your phone from the recipe, with the game's own sounds, and writes a video file to your phone's temporary storage. When it is finished, G asks Photos for permission to add the video, the first time only. If you say yes, G saves the video to your Photos library. G asks for add-only access: it can put a video in your library, and it cannot see, read or change anything else in your Photos.

What G does not keep. G does not keep a copy of the video. The temporary file is deleted when you close the share sheet, when you render again, when you cancel, or when you leave the Replays screen. If you say no to Photos, the video is not saved anywhere, and you can still share it from the Replays screen until you leave it.

What leaves your phone. Nothing, unless you choose to share the video yourself with the share sheet. G does not upload videos, recipes or thumbnails, has no account, and does not use analytics or tracking.

You are in control. You can delete any replay from the shelf by swiping it away. You can turn off G's access to Photos at any time in Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Photos, then G. You can delete a video G saved the same way you delete any video in Photos.

Matches travel through iMessage

When you play a hole and send it, your shot data, score, and golfer name travel inside the message bubble, delivered by Apple's iMessage, the same way a photo you text would be. Only the people in that conversation receive it. Mountain Media Digital has no server and never receives, stores, or can read your matches. iMessage is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Optional one-time unlocks (courses, game formats, colors) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment information. Purchases can be restored on any device signed into the same Apple Account, using Restore Purchases in the app.

Children's privacy

G does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app contains no ads, no links to social media within gameplay, and no data collection of any kind.
