Founder

Kev

Designer · Developer · Mountaineer

Mountain Media Digital is a solo practice run out of La Conner, Washington — at the foot of the North Cascades, between Bellingham and Seattle.

Years of working as a designer in industry led to a simple conviction: the software people actually want is small, quiet, and respects them. So that's what we make.

Outside of the studio you'll find Kev in the mountains — climbing, skiing, or driving the passes that inspired Pass Checker.
