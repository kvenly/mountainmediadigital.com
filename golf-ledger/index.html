01How it works

Speak, review, keep.

One short note after each hole. The app does the counting.

  1. 1  Speak

    Say how the hole went.

    Tap record and talk the way you would to a playing partner: the score, the putts, the clubs. Your words are turned into text on the iPhone, and the audio is never saved.

  2. 2  Review

    Check the hole before it’s saved.

    Score, putts, penalties, fairway and green are filled in from what you said. If the shots don’t add up to the score, it says so and offers a one-tap fix. Change anything, then save.

  3. 3  Keep

    The round adds itself up.

    Every hole goes on the scorecard. The round summary shows your total, putts, fairways, greens, and your best and toughest holes. Share a clean image of the card when you want to.

04Questions

Short answers.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-in.

What happens to my voice?

Speech is turned into text on the iPhone while you record. The audio is never saved. Only the text note is kept with the hole.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone that runs iOS 26. Voice notes work on all of them. On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, short phrases like “Driver, fairway, 4” read best.

What does it cost?

Your first two rounds are free. After that, a one-time $4.99 in-app purchase lets you keep starting new rounds. No subscription. Family Sharing is on. Saved rounds stay readable with or without the unlock.

What language does it understand?

US English.

Is it on the App Store?

Not yet. Golf Ledger is coming soon to the App Store.
