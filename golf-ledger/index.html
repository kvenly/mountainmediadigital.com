How it works

After each hole.

Play a hole, speak a note, and review the round.

  1. 01

    Play a hole

    Play the hole. The note comes after.

  2. 02

    Speak a note

    Say a quick note. Apple’s on-device speech recognition turns your voice into text. The audio is not saved.

  3. 03

    Review your round

    The text notes stay with the round, stored locally on the device.

FAQ

Short answers.

Does it need an account?
No. There is no account.
What happens to the audio?
It is not saved. Apple’s on-device speech recognition runs on the device, and only the text note is kept.
Where are rounds stored?
Locally, on the device. There are no servers, no analytics, and no data collected.

Two rounds are free. Then a one‑time $4.99 unlock through an in-app purchase. There is no subscription. Family Sharing is supported.
