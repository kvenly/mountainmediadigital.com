Golf Ledger

Privacy
Policy.

Effective/November 16, 2026
Overview

Golf Ledger is an iOS app from Mountain Media Digital LLC. The bundle ID is com.mountainmediadigital.golfledger. Everything in the app runs on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.

We do not collect, store off your device, sell, or share personal data. The App Store privacy label for Golf Ledger is Data Not Collected.

What we collect

Nothing. Golf Ledger does not collect personal information.

Microphone and speech

Golf Ledger uses the microphone and Apple’s on-device speech recognition only while you record a hole note. Recognition runs on your device. The audio is not saved. Only the resulting text note is kept.

Stored on your device

Rounds and notes are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

After 2 free rounds, Golf Ledger offers a one-time in-app purchase of $4.99 through Apple. Family Sharing is enabled.

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. Mountain Media Digital never sees your payment information. Refunds go through Apple.

Children’s privacy

Golf Ledger does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version at this address and change the effective date above. If you keep using Golf Ledger after that date, the updated policy applies.
